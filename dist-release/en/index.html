Global Vision

Today, China is impacting the entire world with an unprecedented posture—whether in the waves of economic giants, technological innovation, or cultural output, the global stage cannot ignore this powerful force. However, on the spiritual and religious landscape, the world often sees nothing but a thick mist. You may frequently hear global narratives painting this land as an atheistic desert, or focusing solely on the heavy restrictions, walls, and immense hardships faced by gospel ministries here. Yes, carrying out gospel work in this land is by no means easy. Yet, throughout the history of biblical redemption, whenever God's people faced the most severe oppression and trials in the wilderness, it was precisely then that the Lord of Hosts was about to launch a historic and massive revival amidst the crisis! Here, there are countless Christians whose great faith has been refined through great persecution; here, there are thousands of house churches that have stood faithfully for over three generations. Their faith is not a flower grown in a greenhouse, but pure gold refined in the blazing furnace. In this magnificent modern revival movement, Chinese Christians are standing up under the anointing of the Holy Spirit—we are not only watching and serving in this land, but are being sent forth to the nations, deeply rooting and penetrating every sphere of the earth, preparing to bring down the glory of the Kingdom!

Exodus 1:12 — But the more they were oppressed, the more they multiplied and the more they spread abroad.

Kingdom Strategy: Our mission does not just take place within the walls of a church building, but within the very marketplace of our generation. Facing the new generation of young people, our core Kingdom strategy is: to establish the culture and life patterns that fully belong to the Kingdom of Heaven at the very frontlines of the Marketplace and commerce.

Matthew 5:14-16 — “You are the light of the world. A city set on a hill cannot be hidden... In the same way, let your light shine before others, so that they may see your good works and give glory to your Father who is in heaven.”

In the marketplace, we become the salt and light of the world through exceptional professional excellence, righteous commercial integrity, and living stories of testimony. We must enter the upstream of culture, pioneering in the tracks of technology, creativity, and industry. By leveraging Kingdom values, we aim to reclaim, impact, and shape the drifting younger generation, turning the marketplace into the primary mission field.

Action: Unity in Prayer & Financial Mutual Aid

Unity at the Altar: We must maintain a high level of unity in the deep intercession of churches and fellowships, breaking down geographical limitations, and continuously seeking the Lord’s latest timely guidance for cross-border missions and technological empowerment before the altar.

Mutual Aid in Finance: We must build a solid network of Kingdom supply, demand, and offering. We will fully support, comfort, and help brothers and sisters facing trials, persecution, and lack by sharing financial, material, and supply chain resources. Through practical, tangible Kingdom love, we shall bear witness to that unshakable Kingdom.
